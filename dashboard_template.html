Spotlight: Quantum — Your 2021 Vintage

Quantum never rises above 6% of coverage in any era we can measure — and sits at 1% of 2026 posts — yet it keeps producing the largest checks in the room. It never won the attention war. It won the check-size war.

  • 2018–20 — The science project. Cap tables are sovereign wealth, intelligence-community money (In-Q-Tel) and strategics. Generalist VC is absent; the press treats quantum as a lab curiosity. PsiQuantum quietly raises ~$215M by end of 2020.
  • 2021 — The herd moment (your vintage). PsiQuantum closes a $450M Series D led by BlackRock at a $3.15B valuation (July). IonQ becomes the first quantum IPO via SPAC (~$2B, October); Rigetti follows with a ~$1.5B SPAC. Quantum is suddenly an asset class.
  • 2022–24 — The winter. SPACs crater, capital turns selective, investors demand technical proof over roadmaps. The tourists leave; the sovereigns and strategics stay.
  • 2025 — The record year. Global quantum VC hits $4.9B (QED-C) — more than double the previous record. PsiQuantum raises $1B, the largest private quantum round ever, at $7B. Quantinuum raises $600M at $10B. NVIDIA's NVentures invests in three quantum hardware companies in a single week.
  • 2026 — Still moving, different animal. In this tracker's own window: Universal Quantum's $100M Series A (Oct 8), a $150M U.S. Department of War loan commitment for PsiQuantum (Oct 9), Mesa Quantum's $11.8M — and Quantinuum is now public on Nasdaq (IPO June 2026).
The verdict

Quantum is still a trend — a mutated one. 2021's generalist froth became sovereign and strategic capital writing fewer, far larger checks into hardware and fault tolerance. The herd's attention moved on; the money didn't.

The pattern to sell

Every theme on this page has a half-life. Crypto held ~40% of coverage for years, then fell to 3%. Cannabis spiked to 27% and went to zero. The archive is a graveyard with dates on the tombstones — and knowing when a thesis peaked is worth money to anyone about to pitch into one.
