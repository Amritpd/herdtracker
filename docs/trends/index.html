Spotlight: Quantum — The Control Group

  • Every era, 1–6%. Quantum's coverage share never spiked and never died — the one thesis in the archive that never joined a stampede.
  • 2018–20 — the science project. Cap tables were sovereign wealth, intelligence-community money (In-Q-Tel) and strategics. Generalist VC was absent.
  • The pattern to sell: knowing when a thesis peaked is worth money to anyone about to pitch into one. The archive is a graveyard with dates on the tombstones.
