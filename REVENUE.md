# Herd Tracker — How Dollars Actually Get Extracted
Written Oct 23, 2026. All $ figures are rough planning estimates, not forecasts.
Costs stay ~$0 (pipeline) + ~$12/yr domain, so nearly everything is margin minus Stripe fees.

## The core principle
Nobody pays for the raw data (it's free upstream). People pay for:
1. Access to the AUDIENCE (sponsors)
2. Time saved at a high-stakes MOMENT (founders mid-raise)
3. An ongoing WORKFLOW (subscribers: alerts, bookmarks, CSV)
4. The FEED itself as sales fuel (B2B data buyers)

## Ranked revenue ladder (by $ per unit of effort)

### 1. Direct sponsorship — likely first real dollars
- One sponsor slot in the Weekly Herd Report + the site's print "Advertisement" box.
- Buyers: anyone selling to founders/VCs — startup banking, law firms, cloud/AI tools,
  accelerators, recruiting, fractional CFO firms.
- Rough pricing: $250-1,000 / week once the report has a few thousand readers;
  even a tiny list can trial at $250/mo for one founding sponsor.
- Why first: ONE sale beats hundreds of small transactions. Sold manually by DM/email.
- AdSense comparison (why it's gravy, not the engine): finance RPM ~$20-40 per 1,000
  pageviews (rough) -> 1K pageviews/mo = $20-40/mo; 10K = $200-400/mo. Turns on after
  domain + traffic, then runs itself.

### 2. Founder packs — highest willingness to pay per person
- Raise Targeting Pack: Fresh Powder filtered to sector/stage/region + fund focus fit.
  Sector Dossier: wave history, median rounds, top funds for a sector.
- Rough pricing: $49-99 per pack (one right investor meeting is worth 100x that to a
  founder mid-raise; do not price this at $5).
- KEY: no paywall build needed to start. Stripe Payment Link + concierge fulfilment
  (generate/deliver the pack manually) = zero-code revenue test. Automate only if it sells.
- Rough math: 20 packs/mo x $49 = ~$1K/mo.

### 3. Pro subscription — slow, steady, compounding
- $12/mo or $99/yr: full Archive, full Fresh Powder, all-time Explorer + CSV,
  synced bookmarks + raise alerts.
- Buyers: VC associates/scouts, analysts, corp dev, journalists, founder-job-seekers.
- Rough math: 100 subs x $99 = $9.9K/yr. Retention lives on alerts, not the archive.

### 4. B2B data feed — the sleeper, biggest tickets
- "Just-raised" is a proven sales signal: newly funded companies have money and are buying.
  Vendors (recruiting, dev shops, PR agencies, SDR/sales tools, fractional services)
  pay for early, clean, sector/region-sliced funding leads.
- Product: weekly CSV/API feed of new rounds + new funds, sliced by region/sector.
- Rough pricing: $200-500/mo per customer; 3-5 customers = $600-2,500/mo at ~zero
  marginal cost — it's the same pipeline output, repackaged.
- Pilot path: give 2-3 vendors one free month, then convert.

### 5. The indirect dollars (don't underrate)
- A publication VCs read is network + deal flow + credibility for Amrit's own
  raises and job hunt (his stated #1 bottleneck: capital access). The audience is
  an asset even at $0 direct revenue.

## Sequence
- Phase 0 (now): launch free, EMAIL CAPTURE on everything. The list is the asset
  every later dollar is sold against.
- Phase 1 (first weeks): 1 founding sponsor (manual sale) + Payment-Link founder
  packs (concierge). First dollars without building a paywall.
- Phase 2 (with traffic): flip Pro gate, AdSense on, B2B feed pilot.
- Honest ceiling check: realistic path is $0 -> a few hundred/mo -> low thousands/mo
  if distribution works. Internet money, not F-U money — at ~$0 cost, that's fine.
