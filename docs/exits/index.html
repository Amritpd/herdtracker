Who Got Screwed

Two ways to lose. The loud way: shutdown — 13 in the archive, every dollar of preference stack worthless. The quiet way: an acquisition with undisclosed terms — 65% of acquisitions here. Undisclosed occasionally means "too big to say"; far more often it means acqui-hire math, where the team lands softly and the investors' stack does not. The number they won't print is the number that tells the story.
